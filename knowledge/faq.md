# Digi Store — Customer Support FAQ

## What does Digi Store sell?

Digi Store is an online shop with several departments: electronics (backup
drives, peripherals, headphones, monitors), clothing, books and e-books,
home & kitchen, gift cards, and our own digital services — the Acme Cloud
Storage plans and storage add-ons. Prices, stock levels and product details
change often, so the support assistant always checks the live catalog for
those rather than relying on this FAQ.

## How do I place an order?

Sign in (or create a free account), choose the products and quantities you
want, and place the order. You can order items from different departments
in one order. The price you pay is the price shown when you place the order,
and it is locked in: a later price change, up or down, does not alter an
order you have already placed. You can order up to 100 of any one item per
order.

## What do the order statuses mean?

- **Pending** — we have received your order and reserved the stock for it.
  You can still cancel it.
- **Processing** — your order is being prepared or shipped. It can no longer
  be cancelled online.
- **Completed** — physical items have been delivered and digital items
  activated or sent.
- **Cancelled** — the order was cancelled and nothing will be charged or
  shipped.

Signed-in customers can ask the support assistant about the status of any of
their own orders.

## Can I cancel or change my order?

You can cancel an order yourself while it is still **pending**; the items go
straight back into stock. Once an order is **processing** it can't be
cancelled or edited online. Contact support@digistore.example as soon as
possible and we'll try to stop it; otherwise you can return the items after
delivery (see the returns policy). To change an order, cancel it while it's
pending and place a new one.

## What if an item is out of stock?

Items marked out of stock can't be ordered — we don't take backorders, so
you will never be charged for something we can't send. Popular items are
usually restocked within two to three weeks; check back or ask the support
assistant. Digital products (cloud storage plans, e-books, e-gift cards)
never run out.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Digi Store gift cards. All
prices are in US dollars. Any sales tax or import duty for your address is
shown before you confirm the order. Your card is charged when the order is
placed; if the order is cancelled, the charge is refunded in full.

## How much is shipping and how long does it take?

Physical items ship worldwide from our warehouse.
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days in the US, 7–14 business days
  internationally. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days in the US for $14.99.

Orders placed before 2 pm ET on a business day usually leave the warehouse
the same day. You'll receive a tracking link by email once the order is
processing.

## How are digital products delivered?

Digital products arrive instantly, with no shipping charge. Cloud storage
plans and add-ons are activated on your account as soon as the order
completes. E-books appear in your account library and are sent to your email.
E-gift cards are emailed within minutes, either to you or to the recipient
you choose.

## What is your returns policy?

Most physical items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full
refund, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging. Clothing
must be unworn with tags attached. To start a return, open a support ticket
from your account or email support@digistore.example with your order number.
Return shipping is free for faulty or incorrect items; otherwise a $4.99
return label is deducted from the refund.

These can't be returned: e-books once downloaded, gift cards, and opened
headphones or other in-ear and over-ear audio (for hygiene reasons) unless
they are faulty.

## When will I get my refund?

Once a return reaches our warehouse we inspect it within 3 business days and
refund the original payment method. Card and PayPal refunds usually appear
within 5–10 business days after that, depending on your bank. Refunds paid
with a gift card go back onto a gift card.

## Can I exchange clothing for a different size?

Yes. Each size and colour is its own item in our catalog, so the quickest
way is to order the size you want and return the original within 30 days.
Our clothing comes in sizes XS to XXL, plus "One size" for items like caps
and socks, and each item says whether it's a men's, women's or unisex fit.
If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for hoodies and jackets.

## Do electronics come with a warranty?

Yes. Every electronics product lists its warranty period (usually 12 to 36
months from delivery) in its product details. The warranty covers
manufacturing faults, not accidental damage. If an item stops working,
open a support ticket with your order number and a short description of
the problem; we'll arrange a repair, replacement or refund. Faulty items
within 30 days of delivery are always replaced or refunded, whichever you
prefer.

## How do gift cards work?

Digi Store gift cards can be spent on anything in the store, including cloud
storage plans. E-gift cards are delivered by email; physical gift cards are
posted. Each card is valid for the period shown in its details (currently
24 months from purchase). Any unspent balance stays on the card for the
next order. Gift cards can't be exchanged for cash, returned, or replaced if
lost, so treat them like cash.

## How do I create an account or reset my password?

Create a free account with your name, email address and a password of at
least 8 characters. If you forget your password, click "Forgot password" on
the sign-in screen and enter your email address; you'll receive a reset
link valid for 30 minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam
folder before requesting a new one.

## Why do I need to sign in to ask about my orders?

For your privacy, the support assistant can only see the orders and support
tickets of the customer who is signed in — never anyone else's, even if you
give their email address or an order number. General questions about
products, shipping and policies can be answered without signing in.

## How do I contact a human?

Open a support ticket from your account, or email support@digistore.example
with your order number if your question is about an order. We reply within
one business day. You can track your tickets, and their status, from your
account or by asking the support assistant while signed in.

## What are the Acme Cloud Storage plans?

Acme Cloud Storage is our online backup and file-sharing service, sold in
the Digital services department. The plans (Free, Plus and Pro, with Pro
also available billed annually) differ in storage space and number of
devices; storage add-ons can be bought on top of any plan. All plans
include automatic daily backups and 256-bit encryption, and there are apps
for iOS and Android ("Acme Cloud" in your app store) with automatic photo
backup. Ask the support assistant for current plan prices.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my cloud storage plan?

Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately.
Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You will not
be charged twice in the same month. If you go over your plan's storage
limit, uploads pause until you free up space or upgrade. We never charge
overage fees automatically.

## Can I get a refund on a cloud storage subscription?

New subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase.
Refunds for renewals are evaluated case by case. Contact
support@digistore.example with your account email and the reason. If you
cancel, your files stay available in read-only mode for 30 days and are
then permanently deleted, so download a local copy first if you want to
keep them.

## Can I share cloud files with people who don't have an account?

Yes. Right-click any file or folder and choose "Share Link." You can make
the link view-only or allow editing, and you can add an expiry date or a
password.
