# Acme Cloud Storage — Support FAQ

## What plans do you offer?

Acme Cloud Storage has three plans: Free (5GB storage, 1 device),
Plus ($5/month, 100GB storage, 3 devices), and Pro ($15/month,
1TB storage, unlimited devices). All plans include automatic
daily backups and 256-bit encryption.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect
immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next
billing cycle. You will not be charged twice in the same month.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot password" on the login screen and enter your
email address. You will receive a reset link valid for 30
minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder
before requesting a new one.

## Can I share files with people who don't have an account?

Yes. Right-click any file or folder and choose "Share Link."
You can set the link to be view-only or edit-enabled, and you
can add an expiration date or password to the link.

## What happens to my files if I cancel my subscription?

Your files remain accessible in read-only mode for 30 days
after cancellation on the Free plan tier. After 30 days,
files are permanently deleted. We recommend downloading a
local backup before cancelling if you want to keep your data.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes, Acme Cloud Storage is available on iOS and Android. Search
"Acme Cloud" in your app store. The mobile app supports
automatic photo backup and offline access to starred files.

## How much does exceeding my storage limit cost?

If you exceed your plan's storage limit, uploads are paused
until you free up space or upgrade. We do not charge overage
fees automatically — you must explicitly choose to upgrade.

## Do you offer refunds?

We offer full refunds within 14 days of a new subscription
purchase. Refunds for renewals are evaluated case by case —
contact support@acmecloud.example with your account email
and reason for the request.
